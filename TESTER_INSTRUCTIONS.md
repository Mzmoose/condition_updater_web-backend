# Bulk Photo Downloader – Tester Instructions

1. Open: https://condition-updater-web-backend-1.onrender.com/bulk/ui
2. Click **Sign in with eBay** and complete the consent screen.
3. Enter the **Beginning SKU** and **How many SKUs**.
4. Click **Download ZIP**. Your browser will save a ZIP of photos to your computer.
5. To end your session, click **Sign out**.

If you see “Not signed in,” click **Sign in with eBay** and try again.
